# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith. Argue
about the code, not the person.

## Expected

- Technical disagreement is welcome and useful. Personal attacks are not.
- Assume good faith. Most confusion is a communication failure, not malice.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a contribution for reasons of scope,
  airtime cost, interoperability, or the detection-only boundary described in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Those are engineering judgements, not
  personal ones.
- Remember that some people rely on this firmware in situations where a failure
  matters. Overstating what a change does, or how well it was tested, is a real
  harm here — not a style question.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind
- Publishing others' private information
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Deliberately misrepresenting the reliability or security of a contribution

## Security issues are not conduct issues

Suspected vulnerabilities go privately to **security@blackatlas.tech**, never a
public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope and enforcement

Applies to this repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, and commits.

Report concerns to **conduct@blackatlas.tech**. Reports are handled discreetly.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, or block a contributor, in proportion to
the behaviour. This project is maintained by a very small team; responses may not
be immediate.

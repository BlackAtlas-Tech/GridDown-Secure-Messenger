## Why this exists

Operators need to know whether the firmware on a radio is the one we build,
test, and support. An unofficial build carrying our name in a life-safety
context would be a real problem — for them and for us. Renaming a fork is not
a restriction on your GPL rights; it is how users tell builds apart.
